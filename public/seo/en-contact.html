How to prepare a general legal enquiry for HELM Legal, find public guides and use the secure client portal appropriately.

Preparing a legal enquiry

Before requesting a case-specific review, prepare a concise description of what happened, when it happened, who the parties are, the current procedural stage and the practical outcome you are seeking. A clear timeline helps identify the records needed for an assessment without assuming a claim is legally established.

For general information, browse the UAE legal consultancy guide or learn more about the office.

Existing client files

Authorized users can access the client portal to handle existing files through the approved channels. Not having an active account does not mean sensitive documents should be sent through a public reading page.

Protect confidential information

Do not submit passwords, verification codes, identity documents, bank statements or confidential court records through public links or comments. Before sharing sensitive information, confirm that the recipient and channel are approved by the office.
