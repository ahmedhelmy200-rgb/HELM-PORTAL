Execution and Judgments in the UAE

Understand the distinction between establishing a right and executing an existing instrument.

Identify the instrument

Start with the full enforceable decision or document, its operative terms, named parties and precise duties. The execution stage is governed by what the instrument permits and any applicable judicial rules; it is not a generic repetition of the earlier merits dispute.

Verify the current balance

Check the principal, supported additional sums and enforcement costs against actual receipts or part-payments. Review notices, requests, attachments, inquiries and earlier judicial decisions in the execution record. Later events may change what remains available to request.

Match the next application to the file

A travel restriction, attachment or similar measure requires its legal conditions and an actual decision; it should not be assumed in every case. Obtain an up-to-date file record before acting. Compare judgment enforcement with ordinary debt claims.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
