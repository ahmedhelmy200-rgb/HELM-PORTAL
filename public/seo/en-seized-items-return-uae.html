UAE criminal procedure information

Returning Seized Property in UAE Criminal Cases

A request for the return of an item depends on ownership, evidential need and applicable confiscation decisions.

When the law may permit return

The Arabic source cites Article 78 of the UAE Criminal Procedure Law, which addresses the return of seized property to those entitled to it even before judgment when it is no longer needed for proceedings and is not subject to confiscation. Related provisions and current procedural rules should be verified.

Who may apply?

A person claiming a legally supported interest in the seized object should document that interest, identify the file and explain why a release request is appropriate based on the actual case status. A completed custodial sentence alone does not prove every seized item must be returned.

Documentation checklist

Prepare the complaint or case reference, exact description of the item and serial or other identifier where available, evidence of ownership or lawful possession, relevant judgment if the proceedings have ended and a concise statement of the request. Confiscation, third-party claims or ongoing evidential need may affect the result. Read criminal guides and consult the competent authority.

Important: General legal information only. Verify the original order, current law, competent authority and any strict deadlines. Applications or petitions cannot guarantee a favorable decision or change a restriction without proper authority.
