Egypt Labour Law No. 14 of 2025: Document Guide

A practical overview of employment-file review under Egypt's Labour Law No. 14 of 2025 and its executive measures.

The legislation cited by the Arabic guide

The original article identifies Egypt's Labour Law No. 14 of 2025 as published by the Egyptian Ministry of Labour, alongside executive decisions. It notes an announcement in September 2026 about general rules for private-sector work regulations. Read the current law and associated executive rules; the primary act alone may not cover every practical requirement.

Preparing the employment file

Gather the employment contract, amendments, correct employer identity, payroll and transfers, warnings or notices, and documents concerning resignation, termination or disputed entitlements. Distinguish each period and claim and determine whether another legal regime is relevant.

When the relationship crosses into the UAE

Do not combine Egyptian and UAE employment rules into one undifferentiated claim. Identify where the work was performed, which entity contracted and which legislation applies to each part. Consult Egypt's Ministry of Labour for current publications and read cross-border employment when multiple jurisdictions are involved.

Scope: Public legal information, not case-specific advice. Verify current legislation and procedural competence in Egypt and the UAE separately. Any regulated legal action must be undertaken by a duly authorized professional.
