International Powers of Attorney: Use Across Jurisdictions

Check the person, authority and receiving institution before preparing an international power of attorney.

Identify the parties and purpose

Record the principal, the appointed agent and their capacities exactly as evidenced by official documents. State the particular case, transaction or act to be authorized rather than assuming a broad document suits every process. Determine where the instrument will be executed and where it will be presented.

Check the receiving jurisdiction

The requested action can require specific wording, representation powers, formalities, translation or legalization under local law. The requirements of a registry, court or authority may differ. Verify those rules before arranging costly or unsuitable documents.

UAE and Egypt files

If a power is made in Egypt for use in the UAE, or vice versa, identify both the issuing procedure and the receiving authority. Collect identity documents, case references, corporate authority documents if relevant and the proposed wording for review. See attestation and UAE-Egypt matters.

Scope: General legal information only. Different jurisdictions impose distinct legal and professional requirements. Confirm current rules and involve authorized local advisers where required.
