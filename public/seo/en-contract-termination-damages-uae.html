Termination of Contracts and Damages in the UAE

A contractual default does not necessarily result in rescission or a compensation award.

Grounds for termination

The Arabic source discusses Article 234 of Federal Decree-Law No. 25 of 2025, addressing mutual contracts, failure to perform a due duty, legally relevant notice and possible performance or rescission. Review the precise obligation, date and conduct before choosing a remedy.

Rescission and express terms

The original guide describes judicial discretion to require performance, allow time or refuse rescission depending on the seriousness and remedy of default. It also discusses Article 235 on express termination clauses and the role of notice. The applicable text and agreement govern each case.

Claims for loss

Distinguish an unpaid contract sum from compensation for specific alleged damage. Establish the basis, evidence and causal connection for each claim while acknowledging partial performance and settlements. Read contract-breach proof and civil disputes.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
