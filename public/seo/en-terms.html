Responsible use of legal information

Terms of Use

Terms for using HELM Legal public legal guides and the client portal, including limits of general information and account access.

Public legal information

Articles and guides available on this website are intended for general legal education. Reading them does not create a professional engagement or constitute an opinion on any particular dispute. Relevant facts, documents, current law, competent authority and applicable deadlines must be reviewed before taking action.

Legal and procedural changes

Legislation, regulatory instructions, fees and procedural deadlines can change. Consult the current official version before applying information from an article published earlier.

The client portal

The client portal is intended solely for authorized account holders. Users must not share credentials, attempt to access other people's files, or bypass the permissions granted to them.

Use of published materials

Illustrative examples must not be presented as established facts from court proceedings. Legal explanations should not be quoted without their context so as to imply a guaranteed outcome. See our editorial review policy.

Contact and additional information

For general information, visit Contact and About HELM Legal. Please do not send confidential records through public pages.
