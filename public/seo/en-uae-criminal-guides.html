UAE Criminal and procedural information

UAE Criminal Case Guides

Identify the procedural stage and read the actual decision before considering any application or challenge.

Understand what stage the file has reached

A police complaint, public-prosecution investigation, first-instance judgment, appeal and enforcement stage are different procedural situations. Obtain the correct case number, authority, decisions and available records. Do not rely on an informal description of a judgment when a legal deadline or procedural option is at issue.

Bail, temporary release and criminal appeals

Decisions about provisional release depend on the competent authority, the stage of the case, applicable rules and documented circumstances. A criminal appeal requires careful review of the judgment, its date, how it was issued and the procedure available to the specific appellant. See bail and provisional release and criminal appeal preparation.

Other important procedures

Different applications may be available concerning the return of seized items, rehabilitation, pardon, case-status enquiries, or conditions connected with travel. These procedures should not be treated as interchangeable with an appeal. A request for clemency, for example, does not by itself alter the time limits or legal requirements governing a challenge to the judgment.

Deportation connected to criminal proceedings

Where a decision includes removal from the UAE, the judgment's exact wording matters. A judicial deportation order differs from an administrative decision. Determine the source of the order, the competent authority and any later decisions before considering a lawful application. Read the English deportation overview and judicial deportation guidance.

Document checklist and official sources

  1. Case number, year, authority and procedural stage.
  2. Official copy of the judgment or prosecutorial decision, where available.
  3. Hearing minutes, submissions and relevant evidence already on file.
  4. Documents showing applicable notice or announcement dates.
  5. Written account of the precise legal question and action requested.

The UAE criminal procedure framework includes Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2022 and applicable amendments. Verify the governing rules through official UAE legislation. This is general legal information, not an assessment of an individual case.

Important: This is public information, not case-specific legal advice. Check the actual decision, competent authority, applicable law, procedural stage and current deadlines before acting. No legal outcome is guaranteed.
