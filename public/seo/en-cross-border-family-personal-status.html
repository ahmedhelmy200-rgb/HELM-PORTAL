Cross-border legal coordination

Cross-Border Family and Personal-Status Documents

Handle family-status documents according to the competent authority and law in each country.

Document inventory

Organize marriage, divorce, birth, family relationship and relevant court documents with correct personal data. Identify the country where each record was issued and the past or present residence of the parties, when relevant.

Different legal processes

Family and personal-status procedures can differ significantly between states and sometimes between categories of persons or legal relationships. Identify whether the task concerns recognition, registration, proof of status or a separate substantive right. Do not assume any foreign judgment or record has immediate effect elsewhere.

Translation and authorities

Determine the receiving body's requirements for an official copy, translation, power of attorney or legalization. Use appropriately licensed local professionals when necessary. Related: inheritance and estates, powers of attorney and attestation. This page does not determine any family's legal rights.

Scope: General legal information only. Different jurisdictions impose distinct legal and professional requirements. Confirm current rules and involve authorized local advisers where required.
