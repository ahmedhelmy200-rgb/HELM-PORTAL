Entry Bans and Deportation in the UAE: What Is the Difference?

A travel or entry restriction does not necessarily originate from a deportation judgment.

Judicial deportation

A court-imposed deportation measure appears in a criminal judgment or is governed by an applicable statutory rule. The Arabic source refers to Article 126 of Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2021 concerning Crimes and Penalties, including distinctions between offenses and any special provisions.

Administrative deportation

A separate measure may be taken by competent administrative and residency authorities under immigration legislation and regulations. Its issuing authority and removal procedure can differ from those of a court judgment.

Entry restrictions and lists

An entry restriction can arise from an administrative record or decision without proof that a criminal judgment ordered deportation. Verify the source of the restriction through official records rather than assuming its legal classification from a rejected visa or attempted entry.

How to assess a case

Check whether the operative court judgment contains deportation, whether there is a residency decision or another recorded restriction, and which authority issued it. See deportation overview and lawful return considerations. Do not submit a generic cancellation request without identifying the actual measure.

Important: General legal information only. Verify the original order, current law, competent authority and any strict deadlines. Applications or petitions cannot guarantee a favorable decision or change a restriction without proper authority.
