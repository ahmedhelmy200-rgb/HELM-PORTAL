Economic Courts in Egypt: Jurisdiction and Case Preparation

A guide to identifying specialized economic-court jurisdiction from the governing legislation.

Specialized legislative foundation

The Arabic guide identifies Egypt's Law No. 120 of 2008 establishing economic courts and later amendments, including Law No. 146 of 2019. The official electronic litigation portal lists legislation connected with this specialist jurisdiction. Check the relevant law and current procedural framework.

Do not decide jurisdiction from a company name

A dispute does not automatically belong to an economic court merely because it involves a business or a large amount. Identify the substantive legislation governing the issue and whether it falls within the specialized jurisdiction. Commercial, finance, banking, markets and intellectual-property topics may require different statutory analysis.

Documents and official resources

Gather corporate and transaction documents, contracts, correspondence and any previous judicial documents. Where a published Court of Cassation economic or commercial decision matters, read the full principle and context. Consult the economic-court legislation portal and Cassation judgments.

Scope: Public legal information, not case-specific advice. Verify current legislation and procedural competence in Egypt and the UAE separately. Any regulated legal action must be undertaken by a duly authorized professional.
