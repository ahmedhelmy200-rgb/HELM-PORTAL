UAE Labour Law Consultations

Review the employment relationship, remuneration, termination and claim documents.

Which rules apply?

Examine the employer's entity and governing regime, work contract, offer, changes of role and wage terms. UAE federal private-sector rules apply to many cases, but different arrangements and authorities may have specific procedures. Confirm the legal framework before calculating entitlements.

Separate wages and other benefits

Examine salary transfers, deductions, leave records, the length of service and payments already received. Identify each component of a claim by period and documentary basis. Any gratuity calculation must account for the applicable scheme and qualifying wage.

Termination and complaints

Organize resignation or termination notices, related correspondence and previous settlement or complaint records, and determine the actual stage of the dispute. Review labour disputes, end-of-service benefits and MOHRE guidance where relevant.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
