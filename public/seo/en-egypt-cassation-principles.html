Egyptian Court of Cassation Principles and Judgment Research

How to use the official Court of Cassation judgment search responsibly in legal analysis.

Official judgment research

The original Arabic guide describes the Egyptian Court of Cassation's search for judgments and legal principles across civil, commercial, economic, tenancy, labour, personal-status and criminal topics. A court principle should be traced to its original source rather than reproduced from an unattributed internet excerpt.

Connect a principle to the dispute

Define the exact legal issue, select the appropriate judicial branch, then verify case number, hearing date, chamber and text of the published principle. Compare the principle with the factual and procedural differences in the actual case. A citation without analysis is rarely useful.

Currency and relevance

The source indicates the official platform publishes modern principles, including through 2026; check the current publication and later developments when relying on a decision. The most useful precedent is one that addresses the disputed issue and factual setting, not merely the most circulated quote. Start with the official judgments search and the economic courts guide.

Scope: Public legal information, not case-specific advice. Verify current legislation and procedural competence in Egypt and the UAE separately. Any regulated legal action must be undertaken by a duly authorized professional.
