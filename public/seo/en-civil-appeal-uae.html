Civil and Commercial Appeals in the UAE

An effective appeal identifies a material legal or factual error in the judgment and explains its effect on the outcome.

Understand the relevant appeal period

Article 161 of Federal Decree-Law No. 42 of 2022 concerning Civil Procedure provides a general appeal period of thirty days, and ten days for urgent matters, unless the law prescribes otherwise. Special rules can depend on the type of decision and applicable legislation. The date from which a period begins must be checked against the actual judgment, service and procedural circumstances; this general guide must not be used to calculate a case-specific deadline.

Build an appeal ground from the judgment itself

Read the complete judgment, including its reasoning, rather than the operative order alone. Identify what the court accepted or rejected and compare that finding with the documents and arguments actually submitted. Explain the particular misapplication of law, material evidential misunderstanding, failure to address an influential defense or relevant procedural defect and why correcting it could affect the decision.

Procedural defenses and evidence

Issues concerning service, jurisdiction or party capacity should not be asserted as slogans. Identify the event, document, date and legal consequence alleged. The relevance of additional evidence in an appeal depends on the governing procedural rules and its connection to a specific appeal ground. Avoid adding documents without identifying the fact each is meant to establish.

A structured drafting checklist

  1. Quote or summarize the exact part of the judgment challenged.
  2. Identify the controlling rule or decisive evidence.
  3. Explain the specific conflict with the judgment's reasoning.
  4. Show the material effect on the operative decision.
  5. Formulate the relief requested within the available appellate powers.

Hypothetical payment example

A judgment awards an entire claimed balance despite a previously submitted receipt related to that same debt. The appeal analysis must specify the receipt, amount, date, reason it concerns the same obligation, whether it was properly placed before the court and how it affects the calculation. Merely saying “the court ignored all evidence” is not a developed ground. See the digital evidence guide and check the applicable legislation and judicial procedures.

Important: This guide explains general legal information. The correct route and legal consequence depend on the facts, documents, current applicable legislation and competent authority. The examples are hypothetical.
