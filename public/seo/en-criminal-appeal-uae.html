Criminal Appeals in the UAE

The timing and the nature of the decision must be verified immediately against official documents.

The general appeal period requires case-specific checking

Article 234 of the federal Criminal Procedure Law sets out a general fifteen-day period for filing a criminal appeal, subject to the legal distinctions connected with the appellant, type of judgment and how the decision was issued. Do not calculate a particular deadline from the date someone sent a screenshot of the judgment. Check the operative decision, pronouncement or service and all potentially applicable special rules.

Documents to gather

  1. The full judgment, operative order and reasoning once available.
  2. Case number, year, court and division.
  3. Hearing minutes, memoranda and documentary evidence already submitted.
  4. Official proof of dates relevant to pronouncement or notification.
  5. Any record of defense arguments said to have been misunderstood or overlooked.

Possible areas for legal analysis

Review procedural regularity, adequacy of reasoning, factual attribution, use and assessment of evidence, application of the governing law and any consequential measures in the judgment. The fact that a defendant disagrees with a result does not itself identify a legally effective ground of appeal; the claimed error should be connected to the record and its effect on the outcome.

When the judgment contains deportation

Any accompanying deportation measure must be read from the judgment itself and assessed under the applicable legal rules. It should not be assumed that addressing the main sentence automatically changes a related measure, or that an independent mercy request replaces a lawful appeal.

Illustrative review exercise

If a judgment describes a document as establishing a particular fact, the appeal review would locate that document in the record, identify what it actually states, and explain the alleged material discrepancy. Avoid unsupported claims that all evidence was disregarded. Use the criminal guides hub and check official criminal procedure legislation before acting.

Important: This is public information, not case-specific legal advice. Check the actual decision, competent authority, applicable law, procedural stage and current deadlines before acting. No legal outcome is guaranteed.
