Mediation and Conciliation of UAE Civil and Commercial Disputes

A viable settlement requires precise issues, clearly documented obligations and an appropriate procedure.

The mediation framework

Federal Decree-Law No. 40 of 2023 provides a framework for mediation and conciliation. The source guide refers to implementing decisions discussed in early 2026, including Decision No. 18 of 2025 and Decision No. 710 of 2025 for remote sessions. Verify the current official procedures.

Preparing the issues

Separate agreed transactions from disputed performance or payment. Gather the relevant agreement, invoices, correspondence, contested amounts and possible settlement terms. Mediation can be a structured alternative for some disputes but its suitability and procedure depend on applicable rules.

Record the settlement precisely

Identify the parties, payment amounts, installment dates, required acts, notices and consequences of default. Confirm any steps needed for enforceable effect under the applicable framework. Do not assume a privately signed note is automatically an enforceable instrument. See claims.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
