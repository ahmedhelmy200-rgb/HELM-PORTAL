Civil and Commercial Disputes in the UAE

Translate a commercial disagreement into specific legal questions, evidence and requests.

Common categories of disputes

Civil and commercial files may involve unpaid financial obligations, a failure to perform a contract, liability for loss or damage, and business transactions supported by financial records or correspondence. The label given to a dispute is not enough to determine jurisdiction or the correct procedure; the nature of the parties, requested relief and applicable rules must be examined.

Financial claims and contract performance

Identify the basis of the debt, its maturity and any prior payments. For contract performance, review what was agreed, what was actually delivered, how it was accepted and what objections were raised. A single invoice or transfer cannot always establish every element of the claim or defense. Match each asserted fact with an original document and a date.

Liability and compensation

A claim for compensation requires more than identifying an unpleasant outcome. The analysis should explain the conduct or breach relied on, the harm alleged, the legal basis of responsibility and the connection between them under the applicable law. Separate the principal contractual amount from any additional compensation, interest or expenses.

Building a clear case file

  1. Define the required outcome precisely: payment, performance, termination, compensation or defense.
  2. List each party and its capacity, including any business entity involved.
  3. Arrange all contracts, invoices, bank records, messages and notices in a chronology.
  4. Identify which facts are agreed, disputed or unproven.
  5. Review competent forum, procedural requirements and any previous orders or proceedings.

Illustrative scenario

A customer refuses to pay the last installment of a services agreement, alleging late and incomplete work. The supplier relies on an invoice and delivery emails. Assessment begins with the original scope, the acceptance process, any requests for variation and the payments already made. The example is hypothetical and does not predict the outcome.
