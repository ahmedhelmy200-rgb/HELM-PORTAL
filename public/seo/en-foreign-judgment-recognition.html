Recognition of Foreign Judgments Across Borders

Separate recognition of a judicial decision from steps to recover money or execute an obligation in another state.

What result is requested?

A person may seek recognition of a legal finding, collection of an award or a measure against assets. Requirements depend on the receiving jurisdiction, the nature of the decision and any effective treaty. An authenticated judgment is not automatically enforceable in every country.

Review the full record

Examine the issuing court, party names, service and opportunity for defense, finality or enforceability under the originating system, type of relief, and any subsequent judgments or payments. Criminal, personal-status and ordinary commercial decisions must not be presumed interchangeable.

Documents needed

Obtain a complete official judgment, proof of current procedural status, notices where relevant, receipts showing amounts already satisfied and translations or legalizations as actually required by the receiving authority. Do not spend on certifications before verifying the service's document list.

Cross-border example

A company obtains a payment judgment in one country and later identifies assets in another. The next task is to identify the correct local recognition or execution regime and true outstanding balance, not to assume automatic attachment. See UAE foreign judgments and Egypt-UAE judgments.

Scope: General legal information only. Different jurisdictions impose distinct legal and professional requirements. Confirm current rules and involve authorized local advisers where required.
