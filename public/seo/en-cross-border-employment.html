Cross-Border Employment Contracts and Claims

Work location, the actual employer and contractual documents matter in employment across jurisdictions.

Determine the employment relationship

A worker may sign with one entity, work for an affiliated business abroad and receive wages from yet another account. Identify the contractual employer, where work actually occurred, any secondment or transfer agreement, and which laws or mandatory rules apply. Company group membership alone cannot establish each member's liability.

Essential file checklist

Collect the offer and contract, amendments, payroll records, transfers, assignment letters, assessments, leave or termination notices, correspondence and any existing complaint decisions. Separate entitlement and deductions by period and jurisdiction.

Illustrative group transfer

An employee works in two countries for related companies and disputes the final settlement. Examine whether an actual transfer, new employment contract or temporary assignment occurred and which entity promised each payment. This requires the documents and applicable local regimes; it cannot be resolved solely from the origin of the salary transfer.

UAE and Egypt connection

For UAE procedures see labour disputes and end-of-service benefits. For Egyptian matters see Egypt Labour Law No. 14 of 2025. Local filing and representation rules must be respected.

Scope: General legal information only. Different jurisdictions impose distinct legal and professional requirements. Confirm current rules and involve authorized local advisers where required.
