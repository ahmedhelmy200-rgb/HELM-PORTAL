Dubai · United Arab Emirates

Legal Consultancy for Matters in Dubai

The legal route starts with the nature of the claim and the documents—not simply with the city where one party lives.

Commercial and financial claims

In a Dubai commercial or financial file, identify the legal entity that entered the transaction, the contract or acknowledgment creating the obligation, its maturity and the amount actually unpaid. Compare invoices, transfers, correspondence and any objections to the delivery or performance of services. Whether an ordinary claim, a payment order or execution may be considered depends on the nature of the right, supporting documents and the current rules.

Enforcement-related files

A judgment or existing enforceable instrument must be read alongside the execution records. Determine the operative relief, amounts already recovered, current balance and any orders concerning information requests, attachments or other measures. Do not assume that opening an execution file automatically causes a travel restriction or an asset seizure.

Contracts and business parties

Review the signing authority, company name, deliverables, acceptance milestones, notice and termination clauses, and evidence of any actual breach or loss. A manager appearing in an email should not automatically be treated as personally responsible for the company's commitments.

Illustrative disputed services invoice

A service provider claims the full fee, while a customer says part of the project was never completed. The necessary evidence can include the signed scope of work, stages of delivery, approval records, customer comments, agreed revisions and paid amounts. Separate the sum outstanding under the contract from a distinct claim for compensation. The example is hypothetical.

Preparation checklist

  1. The signed agreement and amendments, with correct party details.
  2. Full correspondence and original digital records.
  3. Invoices and a reconciled payment statement.
  4. Any prior demand, decision or court document.
  5. A dated timeline and the practical remedy sought.

Useful starting points: financial claims, payment orders and commercial disputes.

Scope: The content is general and does not imply a physical office in this emirate or a right of court representation. Review the applicable rules and actual documents before acting. Confidential files belong in authorized channels, not public pages.
