Privacy Policy

HELM Legal privacy information for public legal pages, confidential client files, cookies and potential advertising services.

What this policy covers

This website distinguishes publicly accessible legal information pages from the protected client portal. Client case materials and confidential documents are not displayed as part of the public articles or general informational pages.

Site technologies

The site may use local storage, essential cookies and related technologies for account sessions, settings and operational security. The actual processing depends on the page and functionality used.

Advertising and outside providers

If Google advertising services are activated following approval, Google and its partners may use cookies or comparable technologies to deliver and measure advertisements in accordance with their policies and user settings. You can review Google Ads Settings. Explaining the possibility of advertising does not mean AdSense has approved this website or that ads are currently active.

Client confidentiality

Confidential client records, financial files and private communications are not published on the public pages. The client portal is separate from pages intended for general reading. Access to client files is subject to account controls and permissions.

Questions and updates

Please see our contact information for questions about general website content. This policy may be updated as site services or operating methods change; the version published here is the current one.
