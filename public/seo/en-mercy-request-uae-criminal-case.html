UAE criminal procedure information

Mercy Requests Relating to UAE Criminal Cases

Distinguish a request for clemency from formal criminal appeals and separate deportation procedures.

Before preparing a request

Obtain the judgment, case reference and operative terms. Identify whether the issue is an original sentence, a judicial-deportation measure or another consequence, and determine the current stage of execution. Identify the emirate, prosecution or other competent authority instead of assuming all mercy requests go to the same office.

Relevant supporting facts

Depending on the case and governing procedures, a submission may describe documented family relationships, lawful work, later conduct, relevant humanitarian factors and the status of the other penalties. Do not exaggerate circumstances, omit material facts or present an unverified statement as official evidence.

If deportation is involved

Judicial deportation must be distinguished from administrative deportation under entry and residency law. A request to change a judicial decision is not the same as a submission to an immigration authority. See judicial deportation and requests from Egypt.

Why procedural time limits remain important

An appeal, cassation or other statutory challenge follows its own legal rules and deadlines. A mercy petition does not replace those remedies, automatically stay their deadlines or guarantee acceptance. Check the complete case record and the authority's current conditions before acting.

Important: General legal information only. Verify the original order, current law, competent authority and any strict deadlines. Applications or petitions cannot guarantee a favorable decision or change a restriction without proper authority.
