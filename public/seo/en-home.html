What should you prepare before a review?

  1. Identify the people or companies involved and how they are connected to the transaction.
  2. Create a chronological summary of the material events and the present status of the matter.
  3. Keep the original contract and all amendments, communications, receipts, invoices and formal decisions.
  4. Separate amounts claimed from payments already made, and distinguish agreed facts from disputed issues.
  5. Identify the practical result requested, such as payment, performance, termination, an appeal or action relating to an existing judgment.

These guides are general information, not an individual legal opinion. For current UAE law, consult the official UAE legislation platform and the competent authorities for the matter concerned.
