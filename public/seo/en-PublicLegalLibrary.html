Evidence and Documents

WhatsApp Messages and Bank Transfer Evidence

Distinguish proof of funds moving from proof of why funds were transferred and whether repayment is owed.

  • Match each transfer to banking evidence and the message or agreement describing its purpose.
  • Keep the original complete conversation rather than an isolated admission.
  • Check partial repayment, settlement and the debtor's legal capacity before stating a total.

Which Procedure Fits a Financial Claim?

Establish the source of the debt, correct parties, competent forum and amount before comparing possible claim routes.

  • Identify the transaction and the original documents proving the alleged debt.
  • Separate principal from claimed interest, compensation and procedural charges.
  • Check the statutory conditions for an ordinary claim, payment order or existing enforceable instrument.

Breach of Contract: Proving the Obligation and Loss

Review service or supply agreements, actual performance, delay and documentary support for each sum.

  • Extract obligations, deliverables and dates from signed terms and amendments.
  • Compare actual performance to acceptance reports, delivery records and objections.
  • Explain alleged harm separately from unpaid contractual consideration.

Preparing a Case

Preparing Your Legal File Before a Consultation

Turn scattered messages and documents into a clear case with parties, dates, facts and requested relief.

  • Write a short chronology from the first event to the most recent decision or communication.
  • Gather identity or company details, contracts, receipts, messages and previous formal proceedings.
  • State whether the aim is payment, defense, review of a measure, refund or another specific outcome.

Civil Procedure

When Might a Civil Claim Be Appropriate?

A civil procedure can address a disputed monetary, contractual or other legal right requiring formal assessment.

  • Identify the claimant, respondent and each person's legal capacity.
  • Attach clear documents proving the underlying right and contested facts.
  • Formulate the requested relief precisely and check the proper forum.

Criminal Procedure

Before Reporting a Criminal Matter

A criminal report must concern concrete facts and credible supporting evidence rather than unsupported accusation.

  • Record the date, place and circumstances of the event accurately.
  • Connect each material assertion to a document, communication, relevant witness or other lawful evidence.
  • Use factual, restrained language without exaggeration or attributing unsupported conduct.

Employment

Calculating and Supporting an Employment Claim

Employment claims depend on the governing work regime, remuneration, service period and termination records.

  • Differentiate basic from total remuneration before calculating an entitlement.

  • Break down wages, leave, gratuity and any other claimed amounts individually.

Urgent Legal Matters

What Makes a Legal Matter Urgent?

Urgency relates to actual deadlines, approaching hearings, enforcement decisions, detention or potentially serious legal consequences.

  • Record the earliest known deadline and attach the formal document establishing it.
  • Explain the actual consequence if action is delayed rather than relying on a general claim of urgency.
  • Place the most relevant official decisions and supporting records first for prompt assessment.
