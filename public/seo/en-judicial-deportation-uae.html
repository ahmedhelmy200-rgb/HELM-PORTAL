UAE Criminal and procedural information

Judicial Deportation in the UAE

The wording and procedural status of the criminal judgment control the starting point for review.

Confirm what the judgment actually decided

Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2021 concerning Crimes and Penalties includes provisions addressing deportation in specified circumstances, including Article 126. Some cases involve mandatory consequences while others may involve judicial discretion, subject to statutory rules and exceptions. A general description of a charge does not establish the precise legal status of a particular judgment.

Determine the correct procedural stage

Obtain the case number, court, emirate, degree of judgment, operative deportation wording and available records about finality, appeal and the execution of other measures. Any new decision affecting the judgment should also be reviewed. Do not treat all judicial deportation orders as if they arose under the same circumstances.

Documents for a possible lawful request

Where an application is available, the file may require a written explanation, copy of the judgment, identity and travel documents, previous residency records and relevant documented personal circumstances. The authority may prescribe further documentation, translations or attestations. The supporting records should be accurate and should not omit material information.

Jurisdiction-specific services

The Abu Dhabi Judicial Department publishes a service relating to cancellation of judicial deportation for judgments within the jurisdiction of its courts, with stated documentation and conditions. That is an example of a route linked to the issuing authority; it should not be assumed automatically available for a judgment from every emirate. Verify the currently published service conditions directly.

Differentiate appeal and mercy-related procedures

A mercy request is not another appeal from a judgment and does not inherently suspend legal time limits. Review the actual judgment and current options with the competent authority or authorized legal professional. Related materials: deportation overview and criminal appeals. Reference: Abu Dhabi Judicial Department and official UAE legislation.

Important: This is public information, not case-specific legal advice. Check the actual decision, competent authority, applicable law, procedural stage and current deadlines before acting. No legal outcome is guaranteed.
