Criminal Pardons in the UAE: Different Legal Procedures

Distinguish a specific pardon, a general amnesty, statutory appeal and rehabilitation.

Specific pardon

The Arabic source refers to Article 107 of the UAE Constitution, concerning a pardon or reduction relating to a penalty imposed by a federal judicial authority under the constitutional framework. Competence and rules in local judicial systems must be assessed separately.

General amnesty

The source describes Article 109 of the Constitution as providing that a general amnesty for specified crimes is issued by law. Its legal source and consequences differ from an individual pardon affecting execution of a particular penalty.

Other routes remain distinct

An appeal challenges a judgment under legal time limits; a pardon concerns penalty execution under competent constitutional or other powers; rehabilitation involves statutory consequences following a decision; and a mercy petition requires identifying its authority and basis. None automatically replaces the others or guarantees relief. See mercy requests and rehabilitation.

Important: General legal information only. Verify the original order, current law, competent authority and any strict deadlines. Applications or petitions cannot guarantee a favorable decision or change a restriction without proper authority.
