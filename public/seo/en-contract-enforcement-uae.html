Enforcement of Contractual Obligations in the UAE

Understand agreed duties and reciprocal performance under the governing UAE civil framework.

Applicable legislation

The original Arabic guide identifies Federal Decree-Law No. 25 of 2025 issuing a new Civil Transactions Law as taking effect on 1 June 2026. It discusses Articles 219 and 221 concerning contractual obligations and implementation in accordance with contract terms and good faith. Actual timing and transitional questions need verification.

Reciprocal duties

The source discusses Article 222 concerning reciprocal obligations where one party does not fulfill a due obligation. This issue is assessed against the actual terms, payment schedule, delivery requirements, conditions and each party's performance. It is not enough to claim breach without identifying which duty was due.

Organize the performance record

List obligations, delivery dates, acceptance, objections, notices and part-payments. Separate a demand to perform from termination or damages. See contract review and evidence of breach.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
