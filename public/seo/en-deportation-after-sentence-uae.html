Judicial Deportation After Completion of a Criminal Sentence in the UAE

Review the full judgment and any continuing deportation measure after the original penalty is completed.

The relevant judicial provision

The Arabic source discusses Article 126 of Federal Decree-Law No. 31 of 2021, which addresses judicial deportation of non-citizens in specified criminal cases. A deportation direction can have effect beyond the period of a custodial sentence depending on the law and judgment.

What to check after serving a sentence

Obtain the final judgment with the operative deportation wording, available proof of completion of original penalties, information about any other proceedings and the emirate and prosecution responsible. Distinguish a judicial order from any additional immigration restriction.

Possible requests and relevant services

Some competent judicial departments publish specific procedures for applications relating to judicial deportation. The Abu Dhabi Judicial Department is an example for judgments within its jurisdiction and has distinct criteria and documentation. A mercy request does not guarantee cancellation.

Lawful next steps

Do not treat completion of imprisonment alone as evidence that the person may return freely. Verify the status with the competent authority. Read Abu Dhabi service information, judicial deportation and returning after deportation.

Important: General legal information only. Verify the original order, current law, competent authority and any strict deadlines. Applications or petitions cannot guarantee a favorable decision or change a restriction without proper authority.
