Electronic Evidence in the UAE

The existence of a message is only the beginning: authenticity, attribution, context and relevance may also matter.

What is electronic evidence?

Federal Decree-Law No. 35 of 2022 concerning Evidence in Civil and Commercial Transactions addresses electronic evidence. Article 53 describes evidence derived from electronically created, stored or transmitted data capable of retrieval in an understandable form. Article 54 describes types including electronic records, documents, signatures, seals, correspondence and modern communication methods.

Evidence and the effect of writing

Article 55 addresses the relationship between electronic proof and documentary evidence. Legal weight depends on the nature of the evidence and statutory conditions, including distinctions relating to official and non-official material. It should not be assumed that every screenshot is automatically conclusive or that all digital records have identical proof value.

Preserve the full original record

  1. Keep the original message thread, email file or digital record where possible, not just cropped images.
  2. Record the sender, recipient, dates and the surrounding transaction.
  3. Preserve attachments and related versions rather than omitting replies that change the context.
  4. Link the digital item to the agreement, invoice, transfer, delivery or other relevant event.
  5. Where provenance or integrity is challenged, consider what reliable technical records can be obtained lawfully.

Illustrative example

A business relies on a WhatsApp message saying “I will send the balance” together with a transfer receipt. The analysis asks who authored the message, what balance was being discussed, whether the thread relates to the claimed transaction and whether later payments or settlements changed the obligation. An isolated screenshot does not answer all those questions.
