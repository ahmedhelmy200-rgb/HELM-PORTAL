UAE criminal procedure information

Which UAE Authority Deals With Deportation Matters?

Identify the source and emirate of the decision before selecting a legal request.

Judicial measure

Where a criminal judgment orders deportation, the case begins with the court and competent prosecution or judicial authority. Official UAE guidance describes applications concerning judicial deportation with an explanatory basis and supporting documents, subject to current procedural requirements.

Administrative measure

Where the order is issued under immigration or residency legislation by competent administrative authorities, the relevant residence or immigration channel may be responsible. The requesting process differs from that connected to a criminal judgment.

Abu Dhabi and Dubai examples

The Abu Dhabi Judicial Department publishes a separate judicial-deportation cancellation service for relevant judgments. The official UAE portal also discusses electronic applications in Dubai concerning judicial deportation. Always verify the presently available channel and jurisdiction.

What if the decision type is unknown?

Gather the judgment where available, case details, passport, former residency records and official messages referencing the restriction. The first step is an official assessment of who issued it and whether it is deportation at all. Sending a request to the wrong institution may waste time without affecting the restriction. See entry bans and deportation overview.

Important: General legal information only. Verify the original order, current law, competent authority and any strict deadlines. Applications or petitions cannot guarantee a favorable decision or change a restriction without proper authority.
