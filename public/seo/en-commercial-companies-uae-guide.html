UAE Commercial Companies: Liability and Disputes

Company form, representation and contractual capacity matter in a business claim.

Current company framework

The Arabic guide refers to Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2021 concerning Commercial Companies and amendments including Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2025. Review the consolidated legislation for the actual entity and transaction, including the applicable management and representation rules.

Who owes the obligation?

Check the business licence, constitutional records, signing powers, contracts and any express individual undertaking. A manager or shareholder is not automatically personally responsible for the company's debt merely because their name appears on an email or invoice.

Prepare the commercial evidence

Collect the agreement, amendments, invoices, payments, company status and authorized signatories at the relevant date, and correspondence. Differentiate a debt owed by a company from a shareholder dispute or personal promise. Related: commercial disputes.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
