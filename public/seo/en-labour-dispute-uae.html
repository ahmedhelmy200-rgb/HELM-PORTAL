Employment and Labour Disputes in the UAE

Organize employment entitlements and documentary evidence before choosing the correct complaint procedure.

Confirm the employment regime

Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 regulates many UAE private-sector employment relationships, with relevant regulations and later changes. Before relying on its rules, verify the employer's status, the employee's arrangement and whether a special employment regime applies. Different sectors and jurisdictions may have particular rules or procedures.

Starting a complaint

In applicable private-sector individual disputes, the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation has procedures for receiving and addressing complaints and seeking settlement. The appropriate next step depends on the nature of the claim and the current administrative or judicial stage. Check the official guidance applicable when the complaint is made rather than assuming every file follows the same path.

Documents worth assembling

  1. Employment contract and job offer, including amendments and annexes.
  2. Wage records, bank payments and any statements showing deductions.
  3. Employment notices, correspondence, warnings and performance records linked to the dispute.
  4. Resignation or termination documents and the actual final working date.
  5. Prior complaints, settlement minutes, decisions and calculations, if any.

Calculate each requested entitlement independently

Separate unpaid salary, notice-related amounts, leave entitlements, end-of-service benefits and any other claimed item. State the factual basis and supporting evidence for each entry, as well as payments already received. A total figure unsupported by a breakdown makes a meaningful review difficult. Different rules can apply depending on work arrangement and the governing regime.

Illustrative scenario and sources

An employee disputes the final settlement after contract termination. The file should match salary history, start and end dates, leave information, payroll transfers and the proposed settlement. The calculation is then reviewed under the actually applicable legislation. For end-of-service issues see the dedicated guide. Consult official UAE legislation and MOHRE for current procedures.

Important: This guide explains general legal information. The correct route and legal consequence depend on the facts, documents, current applicable legislation and competent authority. The examples are hypothetical.
