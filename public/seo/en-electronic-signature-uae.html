Electronic Signatures and Digital Agreements in the UAE

Understand how electronic form, identity verification and data integrity affect evidence.

Legal context

Federal Decree-Law No. 46 of 2021 concerning Electronic Transactions and Trust Services regulates electronic documents, signatures and trust services. Article 18 addresses the principle that an electronic format alone does not remove potential evidential value. Always confirm the current statutory text.

Not all signatures are equivalent

A typed name, scanned signature and qualified electronic signature may rely on different technical and legal assurances. Assess how the signatory was identified, whether the record stayed intact and which statutory criteria are met rather than treating all digital signatures as equal.

Preserving evidence

Keep the original file, signing history or audit information if available, acceptance correspondence and relevant contract performance. When attribution is contested, connect the signature to the transaction and the parties' conduct. Related guides: digital evidence and contracts.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
