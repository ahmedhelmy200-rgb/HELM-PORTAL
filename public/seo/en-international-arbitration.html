International Arbitration: Preparing a Cross-Border Case

The complete arbitration clause and transaction history determine how an international dispute is assessed.

Read the arbitration agreement

Collect the full signed contract, incorporated standard terms and any later variations. Identify the exact parties bound, the disputes covered, the institution or rules, seat, language and appointment process. A heading labelled arbitration does not alone establish the clause's validity or scope.

A coherent arbitration file

Prepare the chronology, contractual duties, notices, performance documents, objections, financial ledger and any earlier procedural correspondence. Separate facts from legal submissions and identify the applicable law or forum questions for the relevant professional.

Multiple jurisdictions

A contract can be made in one state, performed in another and involve parties elsewhere. Organizing the file centrally can reduce inconsistent accounts but does not replace specialized local advice or regulated representation. For UAE matters see the UAE arbitration framework; for contracts see international contract review.

Scope: General legal information only. Different jurisdictions impose distinct legal and professional requirements. Confirm current rules and involve authorized local advisers where required.
