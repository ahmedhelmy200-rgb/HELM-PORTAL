Choosing a guide by your legal question

If a customer has not paid an invoice, start with the basis of the claim and the evidence of performance. If a court has already issued a judgment, the task may instead concern execution and the current enforcement file. Where the dispute involves a contract, the questions may turn on scope, delivery, defects, acceptance and any agreed termination process.

The following guides address distinct legal questions. They are for general information and do not replace an assessment of the current UAE rules applicable to your case.

Financial and enforcement matters

  • Financial claims in the UAE: select the appropriate route.
  • Payment orders: documents and procedural assessment.
  • Judgment enforcement: review of the enforceable instrument and balance.
  • WhatsApp messages and bank transfers as evidence.

Contracts, employment and civil litigation

For a contractual disagreement, review the signed agreement and amendments, performance and acceptance evidence, notices and payment records. For an employment disagreement, gather the employment contract, payroll material and termination or complaint documentation. When a decision has already been issued, assess the type of decision and the competent procedure before assuming a particular challenge is available.

Additional source guides in Arabic: contract breach evidence, labour disputes and civil appeals. The original Arabic guides remain accessible; no article is removed by adding English pages.

How to use official sources

For matters subject to UAE law, consult the official UAE legislation platform and any relevant official judicial or governmental information. Check effective dates, scope and jurisdiction. Read our editorial policy for how general guides are reviewed.
