Travel Bans for Debt-Related Matters in the UAE

A debt does not automatically impose a travel restriction; the relevant legal conditions and actual court order must be examined.

Legal context

Federal Decree-Law No. 42 of 2022 concerning Civil Procedure addresses judicial travel restriction requests in debt-related matters, including Article 324. The statutory provisions and their applicable amendments must be reviewed for conditions such as the nature and amount of the debt and substantiated concerns about departure. No general page can establish that a restriction has been issued against a particular person.

Check the grounds and documents

For a proposed application, distinguish the amount and basis of the claim from evidence relied on to support the requested protective measure. Relevant materials may include the underlying obligation, documents about maturity, payment history and evidence relevant to the legal risk criteria. Fear of travel alone should not be presented as a conclusive legal basis.

Before judgment and during execution

The procedural stage matters. A request made before a substantive claim is not identical to a measure sought during enforcement. Determine the responsible court, the available instrument, the documents required, service or notice issues and any existing protective decision. Avoid assuming that all execution files contain a travel ban.

Reviewing an existing restriction

If there is an existing travel restriction, obtain the actual decision and its status. Review payment, settlement, security and other relevant changed circumstances under the statutory provisions. A request to lift an order and a challenge to its original issuance may involve different considerations and procedures.

Illustrative example and caution

A creditor alleges that a matured debt has not been paid and seeks a travel restriction. The correct assessment must verify both the claimed debt and the specific grounds for the proposed measure, rather than use a standard application for every unpaid invoice. For separate debt procedures see financial claims and enforcement of judgments. Confirm current statutory provisions through official legislation.

Important: This guide explains general legal information. The correct route and legal consequence depend on the facts, documents, current applicable legislation and competent authority. The examples are hypothetical.
