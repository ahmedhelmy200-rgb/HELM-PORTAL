Our sources, reviews and limitations

Editorial and Legal Review Policy

How HELM Legal checks official legal sources, prepares explanatory content, reviews updates and keeps confidential client information separate.

Purpose of our content

We prepare public guides to provide general legal information and explain documents, procedures and practical questions clearly. An article is not a case-specific legal opinion: rights, deadlines and jurisdiction depend on the actual facts and documents of each matter.

Sources in order of priority

  1. Applicable legislation published on the official UAE legislation platform.
  2. Published judgments or principles of competent courts when available and relevant.
  3. Official procedural guidance published by the responsible government or judicial authority, within that authority's jurisdiction.

We should not treat a statutory article number or case precedent as definitive without checking its text, source, jurisdiction and effective date when preparing or updating a guide.

Review before publication

We distinguish illustrative examples from verified case facts, identify the scope of a document and explain why it matters. Wording, references and links are reviewed before publication. Examples are hypothetical or general and do not contain confidential client information.

Corrections and updates

Content is reviewed when a meaningful legislative or procedural change is identified, or a material error or omission comes to light. A review date may appear on the article, but the date alone cannot guarantee the legal rule is unchanged when the article is later used.

Limits of reliance

Jurisdictional rules and procedures differ by forum and dispute type. Consult the current governing law and relevant decisions before taking an action. Learn more about the office and our website terms.
