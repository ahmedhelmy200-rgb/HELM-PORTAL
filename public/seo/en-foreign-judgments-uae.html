Foreign Judgment Recognition and Execution in the UAE

A decision from another jurisdiction requires evaluation under UAE law and applicable agreements.

Governing framework

Federal Decree-Law No. 42 of 2022 concerning Civil Procedure addresses foreign court decisions, subject to applicable treaties and other rules. A foreign judgment does not become executable in every jurisdiction simply because a copy has been officially authenticated.

Check the original proceedings

Review the issuing court and its relevant jurisdiction, the procedural status of the judgment, proper notice, an opportunity for defense, possible conflict with relevant judgments, public-policy considerations and any international agreement. The type and origin of the judgment affect the applicable procedure.

Documents and distinct awards

Collect the full decision, reliable evidence of its current status, amounts already paid and required translations or legalizations. Foreign arbitration awards may have a different legal route. Also read cross-border matters and UAE execution.

Important: This is general legal information, not an individual opinion. Review the actual documents, current law and competent authority. No outcome or regulated foreign court representation is promised.
